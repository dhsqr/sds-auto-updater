## 💡 **Pro Tips**

### **LinkedIn Best Practices:**

1. **Post Timing:**
   - Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM IST
   - Higher engagement from decision-makers

2. **Engagement Strategy:**
   - Comment on posts from target companies
   - Share industry safety news
   - Position yourself as expert

3. **Content Mix:**
   - 40% Product demos/features
   - 30% Industry insights/safety news
   - 20% Customer success stories
   - 10% Personal/behind-the-scenes

4. **DM Strategy:**
   - Personalize every message
   - Reference their company/role
   - Lead with value, not features
   - Keep it under 100 words

### **Demo Best Practices:**

1. **Preparation:**
   - Test everything 30 mins before
   - Have backup plan (screenshots/video)
   - Close other tabs/apps
   - Good lighting and audio

2. **Structure:**
   - Start with their pain point (2 min)
   - Show solution (dashboard) (3 min)
   - Walk through one change detection (3 min)
   - Discuss pricing/next steps (2 min)

3. **Closing:**
   - Always ask: "Does this solve your problem?"
   - Offer free trial immediately
   - Set up follow-up meeting
   - Send summary email within 2 hours
